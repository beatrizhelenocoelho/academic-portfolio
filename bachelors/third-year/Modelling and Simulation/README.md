\# Modelling and Simulation



Laboratory projects developed for the Modelling and Simulation course of the Bachelor's Degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico.



\## Overview



The laboratory work focused on building mathematical and numerical models of physical systems and comparing simulated behaviour with experimental data.



Two systems were studied:



1\. A water distribution system

2\. A heat-transfer system



\## Lab 1 — Water Distribution System



The first project involved modelling the evolution of the water level in a reservoir.



A differential equation describing the variation of the water level was derived from the physical system.



The continuous-time model was then converted into a discrete numerical model using Euler's method.



The implementation was used to simulate the evolution of the water level over time and analyse the equilibrium behaviour of the system.



\## Lab 2 — Heat Transfer System



The second project involved modelling a thermal process using two heaters and temperature sensors.



Experimental data was collected from the physical system and used to analyse the thermal interaction between the heaters.



A model was then implemented in Simulink using differential equations describing:



\- Electrical energy converted into heat

\- Heat transfer between components

\- Sensor dynamics

\- Thermal delays



The simulated model was compared with experimental open-loop measurements.



Model parameters were adjusted to improve the agreement between simulation and real measurements.
