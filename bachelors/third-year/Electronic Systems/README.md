\# Electronic Systems



Laboratory projects developed for the Electronic Systems course of the Bachelor's Degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico.



\## Lab 1 — Feedback Amplifier



The first laboratory involved the analysis and implementation of a transistor amplifier with feedback.



The project included:



\- DC operating-point analysis

\- Small-signal transistor modelling

\- Feedback topology analysis

\- Voltage gain calculation

\- Input impedance

\- Output impedance

\- LTspice simulation

\- Experimental validation



\## Lab 2 — Infrared Proximity Detector



The second project involved the development of an infrared proximity-detection circuit.



An NE555 timer was configured as an astable oscillator to drive an infrared emitter.



The reflected infrared signal was detected by a receiving stage and processed in order to estimate the proximity of an object.



The system also included:



\- Infrared transmitter

\- Phototransistor receiver

\- Band-pass filtering

\- Rectification

\- Signal comparison

\- Progressive LED indication based on detected distance



The circuit was analysed theoretically, simulated and tested experimentally.



\## Lab 3 — µOscilloscope



The third project involved the development of software for a small embedded oscilloscope.



The system was implemented in Python for an ESP32-based embedded platform with a TFT display.



The oscilloscope acquires electrical signals through the ADC and displays the waveform in real time.



Implemented functionality includes:



\- ADC signal acquisition

\- Voltage calibration

\- Waveform visualisation

\- Adjustable voltage scale

\- Adjustable time scale

\- Automatic calculation of:

&#x20; - Maximum voltage

&#x20; - Minimum voltage

&#x20; - Average voltage

&#x20; - RMS voltage

\- Frequency-domain analysis

\- Discrete Fourier Transform

\- Digital filtering

\- Wi-Fi functionality

\- Measurement display



A digital Chebyshev band-pass filter was also implemented and tested.
