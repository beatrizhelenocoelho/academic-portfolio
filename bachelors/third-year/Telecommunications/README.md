\# Telecommunications



Laboratory work developed for the Telecommunications course of the Bachelor's Degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico.



\## Lab 1 — FM Stereo Demodulation



The first project focused on the implementation and analysis of an FM stereo demodulator.



The received stereo signal was separated into its different components using signal-processing blocks.



The implemented processing chain included:



\- Low-pass filtering to obtain the mono component

\- Band-pass filtering

\- Extraction of the 19 kHz pilot tone

\- Frequency doubling

\- Coherent detection

\- Channel reconstruction

\- Resampling

\- Delay compensation



The complete processing chain was analysed in both the time and frequency domains.



\## Lab 2 — Digital Modulation



The second laboratory focused on digital communication systems using BPSK and QPSK modulation.



Demodulators for both modulation schemes were analysed and implemented.



The work included:



\- BPSK demodulation

\- QPSK demodulation

\- Constellation diagrams

\- Symbol detection

\- Error analysis

\- Comparison between modulation schemes
