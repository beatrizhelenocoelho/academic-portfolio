\# Physics I



Experimental laboratory work developed during the Physics I course of the

Bachelor's Degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico.



\## Overview



The laboratory component focused on experimental analysis of mechanical systems,

oscillations, energy conservation and wave phenomena.



The work involved experimental measurements, theoretical modelling, data analysis

and comparison between theoretical and experimental results.



\## Laboratory Work



\### Lab 1 — Mass-Spring Oscillations



Experimental study of a mass-spring system under different operating conditions.



The experiment included:



\- Free oscillations

\- Damped oscillations

\- Forced oscillations

\- Determination of oscillation frequencies

\- Estimation of damping parameters

\- Comparison between theoretical and experimental results



\### Lab 2 — Energy Conservation



Experimental study of energy conservation and mechanical motion.



The work included experimental measurements, data processing and comparison

between the experimental behaviour and the theoretical model.



\### Lab 3 — Standing Waves on Vibrating Strings



Study of standing waves and resonance in vibrating strings.



The experiment included:



\- Resonance frequency measurements

\- Wave propagation velocity

\- Influence of string tension

\- Influence of linear mass density

\- Relationship between string length and resonant frequency
