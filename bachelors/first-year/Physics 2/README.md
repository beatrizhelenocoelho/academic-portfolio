\# Physics II



Experimental laboratory work developed during the Physics II course of the

Bachelor's Degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico.



\## Overview



The laboratory work involved experimental investigation of thermodynamic and

modern physics phenomena, combining theoretical models with experimental

measurements and uncertainty analysis.



\## Laboratory Work



\### Lab 1 — Specific Heat Capacity



Experimental determination of the specific heat capacity of different materials,

including copper, lead and glass.



The experiment involved:



\- Heating solid samples

\- Measuring equilibrium temperatures

\- Applying energy conservation

\- Calculating experimental specific heat capacities

\- Comparing experimental values with reference values

\- Analysing sources of experimental error



\### Lab 2 — Photoelectric Effect and Planck's Constant



Experimental observation of the photoelectric effect and estimation of Planck's constant.



The experiment involved:



\- Mercury lamp spectral lines

\- Diffraction of light

\- Measurement of stopping potentials

\- Relationship between light frequency and stopping voltage

\- Estimation of Planck's constant

\- Analysis of the work function
