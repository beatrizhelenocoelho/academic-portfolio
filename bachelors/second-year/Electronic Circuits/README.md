\# Electronic Circuits



Laboratory work developed for the Electronic Circuits course of the Bachelor's Degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico.



\## Lab 1 — Transistor Amplifier



The first laboratory focused on the analysis of a transistor amplifier.



The work included theoretical calculations, LTspice simulation and experimental measurements.



The amplifier was analysed in terms of:



\- Operating point

\- Voltage gain

\- Input impedance

\- Output impedance

\- Frequency response

\- Transistor operating regions

\- Signal distortion

\- Lower and upper cut-off frequencies



\## Lab 2 — Differential Amplifier



The second laboratory focused on the analysis of a differential transistor pair.



The work included:



\- DC operating point analysis

\- Differential gain

\- Common-mode gain

\- Common-mode rejection

\- Input resistance

\- Output characteristics

\- Offset caused by component mismatch



Simulation results were compared with laboratory measurements.



\## Lab 3 — Digital Inverter



The third laboratory analysed the static and dynamic behaviour of a transistor-based digital inverter.



The work included:



\- DC transfer characteristic

\- Logic-high and logic-low output levels

\- Switching thresholds

\- Noise margins

\- Output current behaviour

\- Transition times

\- Dynamic response to square-wave inputs
