\# Automatic Control



Laboratory project developed for the Automatic Control course of the Bachelor's Degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico.



\## Project Objective



The project focused on the modelling and control of a DC motor.



The work was divided into two main parts:



1\. Development and analysis of the motor model

2\. Design and evaluation of a feedback controller



\## Part 1 — DC Motor Modelling



A mathematical model of the DC motor was analysed around an operating point.



The nonlinear motor equations were linearised in order to obtain an incremental model relating the motor input voltage to angular velocity.



Laplace transforms were then used to derive the corresponding transfer function.



The model was analysed through its response to input signals such as step inputs.



\## Part 2 — Feedback Control



The second part introduced negative feedback around the DC motor model.



A controller gain was selected according to performance requirements such as settling time.



The theoretical controller design was compared with simulations using root-locus analysis.



The work included:



\- Closed-loop transfer functions

\- Controller gain calculation

\- Settling-time requirements

\- Root locus

\- Step-response analysis

\- Comparison between theoretical and simulated results
