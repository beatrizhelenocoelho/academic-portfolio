\# Signals and Systems



Practical work developed for the Signals and Systems course of the Bachelor's Degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico.



\## Overview



The work explored fundamental concepts of continuous and discrete signals through numerical experiments and audio-signal analysis.



\## Topics Analysed



The exercises included:



\- Sinusoidal signals

\- Frequency and perceived sound pitch

\- Human audible frequency range

\- Musical-note generation

\- Unit impulse and unit step signals

\- Time transformations

\- Signal sequences

\- Sampling effects



Audio signals were generated and analysed in order to relate mathematical signal properties to real sound perception.



The work also explored the relationship between sampling frequency and the representation of high-frequency signals.
