\# Integrated Electrical Systems of Solar Photovoltaic Technology



Laboratory work and final project developed during the Master's Degree in

Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico.



\## Overview



The course studied photovoltaic-energy systems from individual solar-cell behaviour to the

design and economic evaluation of a complete photovoltaic installation.



\## Laboratory Work



\### Lab 1 — Photovoltaic Module Characterization



The electrical behaviour of photovoltaic modules was experimentally characterized.



I-V and P-V curves were obtained and used to determine:



\- Open-circuit voltage

\- Short-circuit current

\- Maximum power point

\- Fill factor

\- Series resistance

\- Shunt resistance



MATLAB was used to process oscilloscope measurements and generate the characteristic curves.



\### Lab 2 — Temperature Effects



The influence of temperature on photovoltaic performance was experimentally analysed.



A Peltier module was used to control cell temperature.



The resulting I-V and P-V curves were compared at different operating temperatures.



\### Lab 3 — Partial Shading and Bypass Diodes



The effect of non-uniform irradiance on series-connected photovoltaic cells was analysed.



The work studied:



\- Partial shading

\- Reverse bias

\- Hot-spot formation

\- Bypass diodes

\- Multiple power maxima

\- Local and global maximum-power points



The expected behaviour was also analysed using LTspice.



\### Lab 4 — Tilt Angle



The effect of panel inclination and incident radiation angle was measured experimentally.



Tests were performed using multiple:



\- Tilt angles

\- Irradiance levels



The resulting electrical characteristics were compared to evaluate their impact on

photovoltaic performance.



\### Lab 5 — Spectral Dependence



Photovoltaic modules were evaluated under different wavelengths of incident light.



Measurements were performed using:



\- Red light

\- Green light

\- Blue light



The experiment examined the relationship between photon energy, spectral response and

electrical performance.



\## Final Project — PV System for Olhão City Hall



The final project consisted of designing a photovoltaic installation for Olhão City Hall.



The design included:



\- Site characterization

\- Electrical load analysis

\- PV module selection

\- Roof geometry

\- Panel layout

\- Maximum installation capacity

\- PV inverter selection

\- Battery inverter selection

\- Battery sizing

\- Cable sizing

\- Complete system architecture

\- Monthly energy balance

\- Performance analysis

\- Financial analysis

\- Cost-optimal system sizing
