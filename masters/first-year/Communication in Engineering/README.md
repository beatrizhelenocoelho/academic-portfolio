\# Communication in Engineering



Work developed for the Communication in Engineering course of the Master's Degree in

Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico.



\## Technical Case Study — Open Water Channel Modelling



The work used the modelling of an open water distribution channel as the technical subject

for developing engineering communication skills.



The objective of the technical study was to analyse how the water level in an open channel

changes when the opening of a downstream gate is modified.



\## Model



The behaviour of the open channel was represented using a mathematical model based on

fluid-flow dynamics.



The study included:



\- Equilibrium-point analysis

\- Water-flow modelling

\- Saint-Venant equations

\- Numerical simulation

\- Euler integration

\- MATLAB simulation

\- Analysis of the resulting water-level dynamics



\## Communication Deliverables



The technical work was presented through both written and oral communication.



The repository therefore contains:



\- Technical report

\- Presentation

\- Supporting presentation material



The project focused not only on obtaining technical results but also on presenting an

engineering problem, methodology and conclusions clearly to a technical audience.
