\# Modelling and Automation of Industrial Processes



Laboratory work developed for the Modelling and Automation of Industrial Processes course

of the Master's Degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Instituto Superior Técnico.



\## Overview



The laboratory work focused on industrial automation using Programmable Logic Controllers

(PLCs), digital input/output systems, industrial communication and process monitoring.



\## Lab 1 — PLC Digital Inputs and Outputs



The first laboratory introduced the architecture and operation of an industrial PLC.



A Schneider Premium TSX P57 PLC and Unity Pro were used.



The work included:



\- PLC digital input/output identification

\- Physical connection of I/O modules

\- Control of a seven-segment display

\- Generation of output sequences

\- PLC scan-cycle measurement

\- Analysis of digital communication timing

\- RC circuits and input switching delays



MATLAB was also used to process and visualize experimental data collected from the system.



\## Lab 2 — Intrusion Detection Alarm



The second laboratory consisted of implementing an intrusion-detection alarm using the PLC.



The system included:



\- Presence sensor

\- Window sensor

\- Alarm switch

\- Green, yellow and red status LEDs

\- Buzzer

\- 4×3 matrix keypad



Different alarm operating states were implemented using PLC programs and subroutines.



The project also included keypad scanning and recognition.



Users could enter sequences through the keypad to interact with the alarm system.



\## Modbus and Virtual Terminal



A virtual terminal developed in MATLAB was connected to the PLC using Modbus communication.



This allowed:



\- PLC signal monitoring

\- Remote data acquisition

\- Event logging

\- Real-time plotting



\## Industrial Cybersecurity Study



The course also included a study of industrial cybersecurity based on the Stuxnet attack.



The study addressed:



\- Industrial network architectures

\- Industrial security problems

\- Attack techniques

\- Stuxnet operation

\- PLC state manipulation

\- Attack impact

\- Security countermeasures
